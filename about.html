Alignment, applied to supply chains.

Ality Intelligence helps suppliers and project teams plan large supply chains together, in confidence for both sides. The name comes from alignment: bringing what a supplier makes into line with what a project needs.

Why Ality exists

Large energy and infrastructure projects run on long, specialised supply chains that are hard for a new supplier to enter and slow for a project team to widen. We saw it first in offshore wind.

Much of that slowness is caution. A supplier will not share designs and costs with a buyer it does not know, and a project team will not open its requirements to every company that asks. Ality stands between the two and hears both sides in confidence. We keep a shared network of the supply chain, built from public data and expert input, and give each side the same answer about the fit.

How we work

Confidential by default

Neither side sees the other's material. Only the assessment is shared, and each side agrees beforehand what of its material the assessment may draw on.

Collaborative, not adversarial

The supplier and the project team receive the same assessment. We work towards an honest answer for both, not a negotiation one side has to lose.

One shared picture

Both sides' answers come from the same network of the supply chain, built from public data and expert input.

Reasons behind every finding

Where it fits, where it does not, and what would change that, with the reasoning set out.
