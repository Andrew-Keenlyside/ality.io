About Ality

Alignment, applied to supply chains

We name our company after what we do: bring centrality and alignment to the networks behind the energy transition.

Better matches come from sharing what's useful — and protecting what isn't yours to share.

Bringing a new product into a renewable supply chain means two sides have to understand each other: the supplier who makes the part, and the builders assembling turbines, jackets and grid infrastructure. But each side holds information it can't simply hand over — proprietary IP on one side, confidential designs and commercial terms on the other.

Ality was founded to bridge that gap without breaking the confidentiality. We keep a shared knowledge base — a graph built from curated open-source data and closed expert input — that evaluates how well a product fits a given supply chain. Each side works with its own agent, and those agents collaborate against the shared graph while their sensitive data stays on its own side.

The result is a fit-and-risk report both parties can trust, produced through a collaborative, compartmentalised process — not an adversarial one. Our first focus is offshore wind, where the stakes and the complexity are highest; the same approach extends across renewable energy as the transition scales.
