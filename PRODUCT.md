## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no build step; hosted from this repository (GitHub Pages or a custom domain, not yet set up).

## Users

Two sides of an energy supply chain, addressed equally:

- **Suppliers** bringing a product or service into a large energy project's supply chain, who need to show it fits without handing over proprietary detail.
- **Builders**: developers, OEMs and tier-1 contractors who must evaluate and qualify new suppliers without exposing confidential designs, requirements or commercial terms.

Visitors arrive from introductions, events and search, and judge in seconds whether Ality understands their world.

## Product Purpose

Confidential supply chain planning: Ality maps the suppliers, parts and dependencies behind large projects as one network, and tells a supplier and a project team how well a new product fits and where the risks lie. Both sides get the same fit-and-risk assessment. Success: a supplier or a project team gets in touch about their supply chain.

## Positioning

A general tool for confidential supply chain planning (user, 2026-11-18), following the original site's flow: the problem of fitting new work into deep supply chains, confidentiality by design, what is looked at, then "Let's map your network". Do not list sectors the company has no work in (user, 2026-11-19: the sectors band "makes very broad claims"). Each side keeps its sensitive information, and both receive the same assessment. General across energy and infrastructure supply chains; offshore wind is where the founders started, mentioned once or twice, never the frame.

## Operating Context

Large, multi-tier energy supply chains (offshore and onshore wind, grid, solar, hydrogen, storage and wider infrastructure) where supplier qualification is slow and each side guards its information.
